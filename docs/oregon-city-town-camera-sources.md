# Oregon City And Town Camera Sources

This note captures the city and town oriented Oregon sources that are good candidates for v1 or near-term integration.

## Verified public sources

### Salem

- Source: City of Salem traffic camera viewer
- URL: https://www.cityofsalem.net/community/transportation-getting-around/traffic-road-conditions/view-traffic-cameras
- Notes: Official city traffic camera page. City messaging indicates the network has undergone restoration work, so availability should be monitored.

### Newport

- Source: City of Newport live web camera and local weather
- URL: https://www.newportoregon.gov/city_administration/about_newport/kgw_web_camera.php
- Notes: Official city page with webcam and weather context.

### Corvallis

- Source: Oregon State University webcam portal
- URL: https://webcam.oregonstate.edu/
- Notable pages:
  - Monroe Avenue: https://webcam.oregonstate.edu/monroe
  - Library Quad: https://webcam.oregonstate.edu/libraryquad
- Notes: Campus-hosted but useful as city-core views.

### Bend

- Source: Oregon State University webcam portal
- URL: https://webcam.oregonstate.edu/
- Notable pages:
  - OSU Cascades - Innovation District: https://webcam.oregonstate.edu/cascades1
  - OSU Cascades - Bruckner Courtyard: https://webcam.oregonstate.edu/cascades3
- Notes: Strong city context for Bend alongside TripCheck and wildfire sources.

### Newport harbor and bay

- Source: Oregon State University webcam portal
- URL: https://webcam.oregonstate.edu/
- Notable pages:
  - Yaquina Bay: https://webcam.oregonstate.edu/yaquinabay
  - Ship Operations: https://webcam.oregonstate.edu/ships
- Notes: Good harbor and bridge coverage.

### Newport wildlife / harbor activity

- Source: OSU Marine Mammal Institute Sea Lion Webcam
- URL: https://mmi.oregonstate.edu/sea-lion-webcam
- Notes: Official webcam with clear public access.

### Eugene

- Source: TripCheck west Oregon roadside camera report
- URL: https://www.tripcheck.com/DynamicReports/Report/Cameras/4
- Notes: Official TripCheck report explicitly lists Eugene-area cameras including `I-105 at Coburg Rd`, `I-105 at Country Club`, `OR132 Delta Hwy Near Valley River`, `OR569 Beltline at Delta Hwy`, and other nearby corridor views.

- Source: City of Eugene traffic related data page
- URL: https://www.eugene-or.gov/624/Traffic-Related-Data
- Notes: Official city page states the city has cameras located around town, but I have not yet verified a city-operated public viewer URL. For now, the clean public Eugene coverage comes from TripCheck and OHAZ.

- Source: Oregon Hazards Lab media toolkit
- URL: https://ohaz.uoregon.edu/media-toolkit/
- Notes: Official OHAZ page explicitly lists `Axis-UOregon1` and `Axis-UOregon2` current-image endpoints, which gives Eugene verified wildfire-camera coverage.

### Toledo

- Source: TripCheck west Oregon roadside camera report
- URL: https://www.tripcheck.com/DynamicReports/Report/Cameras/4
- Notes: Official TripCheck report lists Toledo-area corridor cameras on `US20 at Dudlee Hill`, `US20 at Pioneer Mountain`, and `US20 at Spring Hill Dr`.

- Source: TripCheck west Oregon weather stations
- URL: https://www.tripcheck.com/DynamicReports/Report/WeatherStations/4
- Notes: Official TripCheck weather station report also confirms Toledo-area corridor points on `US20 at Dudlee Hill`, `US20 at Pioneer Mountain`, and `US20 WB at Spring Hill Dr`.

- Notes: I have not yet verified a Toledo city-owned or Port of Toledo public webcam. The nearest strongly verified public civic coverage remains the Toledo corridor plus Newport/Yaquina Bay cameras.

### Florence and Lane County recreation sites

- Source: Lane County Webcams
- URL: https://apps.lanecounty.org/lcwebcams/
- Notes: Official county webcam hub. Harbor Vista is especially useful for Florence/coast visibility.

## Statewide and regional anchors that also cover towns

- ODOT TripCheck: https://www.tripcheck.com/Pages/API
- Oregon Hazards Lab wildfire cameras: https://ohaz.uoregon.edu/wildfire-cameras/
- PGE WildfireWatch: https://portlandgeneral.wildfirewatch.com/
- Oregon DEQ wait-line cameras: https://www.oregon.gov/deq/vehicle-inspection/pages/web-cameras.aspx
- NWS marine / bar cameras: https://www.weather.gov/mfr/marine
